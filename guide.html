How to Use PyShare

Fast, private, local file transfer and clipboard hub. Connect your PC and phones in seconds with zero configuration.

🚀 Quick Start (PC to Phone in 3 Steps)
1

Connect to the Same Wi-Fi

Make sure both your PC and phone are connected to the same Wi-Fi router or connect your PC to your phone's Personal Hotspot. No internet is required!

2

Scan the QR Code on Your PC Screen

Launch PyShare on your PC. A dynamic QR code will appear on your screen. Point your phone's camera (iPhone Camera or Android Google Lens) at the screen and tap the link to open.

3

Enter the 4-Digit PIN

Look at the PIN CODE displayed on your PC screen and type it on your phone's keypad. The PIN rotates every 30 seconds for security. You are now securely connected!

💡
Zero App Installation Required on ANY Device: Whether on a PC, Mac, iPhone, iPad, or Android, users can run PyShare 100% inside their web browser without downloading or installing any software! And if you do download the desktop app, that works offline with maximum 100 MB/s local speeds!
🌐 3 Ways to Connect Any Device
🏠 Local Wi-Fi Hub ⚡ 50–100 MB/s

Desktop app mode on PC. Both devices on same home Wi-Fi or Hotspot. 100% offline, zero internet consumed, ultra-fast transfers.

🌐 Internet Mode 4G / 5G Anywhere

Click "Enable Internet" on PC. Generates a secure Cloudflare HTTPS tunnel so phones on 4G/5G mobile data can connect from anywhere!

⚡ 100% Web Transfer Zero Downloads

Visit /p2p.html on any browser (PC, Mac, Phone, Tablet). Device A creates a room & PIN; Device B joins. Direct browser-to-browser WebRTC!

✨ Core Features & Superpowers
📤 Sending Files (PC & Phone)
Drag and drop files anywhere onto the dashboard, or tap Browse Files. A floating upload drawer with real-time speed meter (MB/s) and individual progress bars tracks your transfers.
👁️ In-App Media Lightbox Previews
Tap any photo, video, song, or text file to preview it instantly in full resolution! Videos play with smooth HTML5 scrubbing, audio files play in an integrated player, and code/text files include a 1-click Copy Text button.
📋 Universal Shared Clipboard
Tap the 📋 icon in the navbar. Type text or paste links to beam them across all connected devices in real time. On PC, click "Push PC Clipboard" to send your current Windows clipboard with 1 click!
📦 Download All as ZIP
Need to download 20 photos or an entire folder at once? Click the 📦 ZIP All button to stream the whole folder in an optimized, zero-RAM compressed archive.
📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)
On your iPhone (Safari) or Android (Chrome), tap Share → Add to Home Screen. PyShare will install as a full-screen mobile app without URL bars!
❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs
💻 Do I have to download the PC app to use PyShare?
No! You can use PyShare completely from the web on both PC and mobile by opening Web Transfer mode (/p2p.html). No software download is required on any device.
However, downloading the PyShare desktop app gives you extra powers: 100 MB/s local offline Wi-Fi speeds without consuming any internet, automatic file saving to your Desktop folder, and 1-click Windows clipboard sync. Both work seamlessly!
⚠️ My phone says "Site can't be reached" after scanning QR code?
1. Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi as your PC.
2. If you are on university/college, airport, or hotel Wi-Fi, the router likely has AP Isolation (Guest Mode) turned on. Solution: Turn on your phone's Personal Hotspot and connect your PC to it, or click "🌐 Enable Internet" on your PC!
🔑 Why does the PIN keep changing?
The 4-digit PIN rotates every 30 seconds to prevent unauthorized devices on your Wi-Fi from guessing it. PyShare includes a 30-second dual-grace window, so if the PIN changes while you are typing it, your login still succeeds!
📂 Where are received files saved on my PC?
All files uploaded from phones are automatically saved into: Desktop/PyShare_Files/Received_Files. Files you place inside Shared_Files are immediately visible for connected phones to download.
🛑 How do I shut down the server?
Click the red 🛑 button in the top right corner of the host dashboard, or press Ctrl + C in your terminal. All active connections and tunnels will close safely.
