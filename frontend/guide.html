How to Use PyShare

Fast, private, local file transfer and clipboard hub. Featuring direct drag & drop file staging, standalone native desktop app (PC), and zero-install web transfer (iPhone, Android, Mac, Linux).

Quick Start (Host to Client in 3 Steps)
1

Connect to the Same Wi-Fi

Make sure both the Host and Client devices are connected to the same Wi-Fi router or connect to a device's Personal Hotspot. No internet is required!

2

Scan the QR Code on the Host Screen

Launch PyShare Host on your device (double-click the standalone PyShare.exe desktop app on PC, or open Host Mode in your browser). A dynamic QR code will appear on the Host screen. Point the Client device camera (iPhone Camera or Android Google Lens) at the screen and tap the link to open.

3

Enter the 4-Digit PIN

Look at the PIN CODE displayed on the Host screen and type it on the Client device keypad. The PIN rotates every 30 seconds for security. You are now securely connected!

Two Seamless Ways to Use PyShare:
• Instant Web Mode: Run 100% inside any browser on PC, Mac, iPhone, iPad, or Android with zero software downloads.
• Standalone Native Desktop App (PyShare.exe): Runs in its own dedicated Cyberpunk Neon Glass window with zero browser dependencies, 100% offline local transfers up to 100 MB/s (works with Mobile Data OFF), and zero-copy direct drag & drop file staging!
3 Ways to Connect Any Device
Native Desktop App 50–100 MB/s

Standalone PyShare.exe on PC. Runs natively in its own window. Dual Host & Client modes, direct file staging, and 100% offline local Wi-Fi transfers with Mobile Data OFF.

Internet Mode 4G / 5G Anywhere

Click "Enable Internet" on PC. Generates a secure Cloudflare HTTPS tunnel so phones on 4G/5G mobile data can connect from anywhere!

Visit pyshare.vercel.app on any browser. Uses ~50 KB internet for the room PIN handshake, then streams files directly P2P locally over Wi-Fi with 0 MB internet data consumed!

Core Features & Superpowers
Direct File Staging (No Folder Duplication!)
In PyShare, you do not need to copy files into a shared folder or expose your hard drive. On Host (Desktop App or Web), simply drag & drop files or folders from anywhere on your PC into the "Add to Shared" dropzone, or click "Select Files" / "Select Folders".
Your files are staged on-demand directly from their original location on disk without duplicating storage. You can add more files or remove staged items anytime with 1 click!
Receiving Files & Custom Download Folder
When client devices send files to your Host PC, they are automatically saved into your designated Received_Files folder (default: Desktop/PyShare_Files/Received_Files).
In the Desktop App, you can click Change to choose any download folder on your computer, and click Open to view received files instantly in Windows File Explorer.
Dual Mode Desktop App (Host & Client)
The standalone desktop app gives you two modes in one window:
• Host Mode: Generates a local transfer hub with dynamic QR code, rotating PIN, 1-click clipboard push, and direct file staging.
• Client Mode: Connect to any other PyShare host on your network. Search shared files with real-time filtering, preview media, download directly to your PC, or send files to the host!
In-App Media Lightbox Previews
Tap any photo, video, song, or text file to preview it instantly in full resolution! Videos play with smooth HTML5 scrubbing, audio files play in an integrated player, and code/text files include a 1-click Copy Text button.
Universal Shared Clipboard
Tap the clipboard icon in the navbar. Type text or paste links to beam them across all connected devices in real time. On Host, click "Push Host Clipboard" to send your clipboard with 1 click!
Download All as ZIP
Need to download 20 photos or an entire folder at once? Click the ZIP All button to stream the whole folder in an optimized, zero-RAM compressed archive.
Progressive Web App (PWA)
On your iPhone (Safari) or Android (Chrome), tap Share → Add to Home Screen. PyShare will install as a full-screen mobile app without URL bars!
Troubleshooting & FAQs
Do I have to download the PC app to use PyShare?
No! You can use PyShare completely from the web on both PC and mobile by opening Web Transfer mode (pyshare.vercel.app). No software download is required on any device.
However, downloading the standalone PyShare Desktop App (PyShare.exe) gives you extra powers: runs completely in its own native Cyberpunk Glass window (zero browser dependencies or tabs), 100 MB/s local offline Wi-Fi speeds without consuming any internet, direct file staging from anywhere on your PC, customizable download folder for received files, and 1-click Windows clipboard sync.
Why is there no "Open Folder" or "Change Folder" for choosing files to share?
Because PyShare uses its own Direct Staging architecture! Unlike older file servers that require you to put all your files into a dedicated shared directory or expose entire folders, PyShare lets you pick and stage files directly: just drag & drop or click "Select Files" to stage individual files or folders from anywhere on your PC without copying or moving them.

The "📁 Change" and "📂 Open" buttons exist strictly on the receiving/downloading side so you can configure where incoming files sent by client devices are stored on your computer.
Does the Web version consume mobile data when sending large files (e.g., 3 GB)?
No, absolutely not! When both devices are on the same Wi-Fi router or Phone Hotspot, PyShare's WebRTC engine transfers the file directly between devices over local Wi-Fi radio waves.
Internet data is only used for the initial 2-second PIN handshake (~50 KB to 100 KB). Transferring a 3 GB, 5 GB, or 10 GB file consumes 0 MB of your mobile data balance!
How do I share files 100% offline with Mobile Data turned OFF?
If you have no internet or want your mobile data completely OFF, use the Native Desktop App:
1. Turn on your Phone Hotspot (Mobile Data can stay OFF).
2. Connect your PC to the Phone Hotspot Wi-Fi.
3. Double-click PyShare.exe on your PC.
4. Drag and drop the files you want to share into the "Add to Shared" dropzone.
5. Scan the QR code or enter the local IP shown on the PC screen (e.g. http://192.168.43.xxx:8010) in your phone browser.
6. Enter the 4-digit PIN. Your phone connects directly to your PC's offline hub with zero internet needed!
My phone says "Site can't be reached" after scanning QR code?
1. Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi as your PC.
2. If you are on university/college, airport, or hotel Wi-Fi, the router likely has AP Isolation (Guest Mode) turned on. Solution: Turn on your phone's Personal Hotspot and connect your PC to it, or click "🌐 Enable Internet" on your PC!
Why does the PIN keep changing?
The 4-digit PIN rotates every 30 seconds to prevent unauthorized devices on your Wi-Fi from guessing it. PyShare includes a 30-second dual-grace window, so if the PIN changes while you are typing it, your login still succeeds!
Where are received files saved on the Host?
All files sent to the Host are automatically saved into: Desktop/PyShare_Files/Received_Files by default.
In the Desktop App, you can change this anytime by clicking Change, or open the folder immediately with Open. Files you choose to share from the Host are staged directly from their current location on-the-fly without being copied into a shared directory.
How do I shut down the server?
Click the red Stop button in the top right corner of the host dashboard, or press Ctrl + C in your terminal. All active connections and tunnels will close safely.
